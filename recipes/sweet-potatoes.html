Hot Honey Sweet Potatoes with Whipped Feta and Pistachios

Description

This sweet potatoe with whipped feta and pistachio recipe is melt-in-your-mouth good. The whipped feta adds a layer of creaminess to the roasted sweet potatoe.

To top it off, this recipe is garnished with pistachios to add an extra crunch to the cloudy texture of the whipped feta. It's a recipe that will surely turn heads.

Ingredients

  • 8 lb sweet potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1 1/2-inch wedges
  • 1/3 cup olive oil, 2 tsp kosher salt, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp ground cumin
  • Hot honey: 3/4 cup honey, 1 tbsp red pepper flakes (or 2 tbsp Calabrian chili paste), 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar, 2 tbsp butter, pinch of salt
  • Whipped feta: 12 oz feta (drained), 8 oz cream cheese, ¼ cup olive oil, juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove
  • To finish: 3/4 cup chopped roasted pistachios, 1 cup pomegranate arils, 1/2 cup chopped cilantro or mint

Steps

  1. Hot honey: Simmer everything over low heat 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened. Cool and store in a jar.
  2. Whipped feta: Blend the feta, cream cheese, oil, lemon juice, and garlic in a food processor 2–3 minutes until completely smooth and fluffy. Refrigerate.
  3. Toss the wedges with the oil, salt, and spices. Spread in a single layer on 2–3 rimmed sheet pans (don't crowd them or they'll steam).
  4. Roast at 450°F, flipping once, 35–40 minutes until browned and tender.
  5. Spread the whipped feta across a large platter. Pile the potatoes on top and drizzle with hot honey. Scatter with pistachios, pomegranate, and herbs.
